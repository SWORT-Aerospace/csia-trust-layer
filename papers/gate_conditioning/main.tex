\section{Scope of the mathematical paper}

The paper concerns \emph{gate conditioning}: the statistical consequences of a
validity gate whose closure depends on the vehicle state, potentially
including the state produced by the fault under diagnosis. The distinction the
paper turns on is between \emph{endogenous} coarsening, where a gate closes as
a function of that state, and \emph{exogenous} coarsening, where a channel is
absent from the record for reasons unrelated to the fault. The coarsening
index $\kappa$ is agnostic to which mechanism applies; results that turn on
the cause of unavailability apply only to the first.
