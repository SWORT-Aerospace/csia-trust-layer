## 3. WHY "HISTORY-DOMINATED" IS NOT USED FOR THE CURRENT-STACK RESULT

Post-hoc sensitivity over the recorded Mechanism B traces
(`research/v1_1/results/s0_sensitivity_posthoc.json`), holding the measured
per-tick evidence and forgetting streams fixed and varying only the initial
banked state:

| initial banked state | crossing tick |
|---|---|
| S0 = 0 (no history) | 52 to 58 |
| 0.25 x S0 | 72 to 78 |
| 0.50 x S0 | 82 to 88 |
| S0 measured (133 to 136) | 94 to 99 |

Monotone in all five trials. Banked history accounts for roughly 42 of about 96
ticks: a large **minority**, not a majority. The current-stack result is
therefore named a **threshold-crossing persistence contrast**. Phase-I retains
**history-dominated threshold crossing** as its own analytical result for its
own measured parameter set.
