# Evidence provenance audit (key question 6)

Closed-loop evidence gap search, 2026-09-28. Source ids `Lnnn` are defined in
`research/closed_loop_evidence_gap/PROBLEM_LANDSCAPE.md` §2.

**The question.** Do monitors represent why an observation exists, and
which autonomy actions influenced it? "Evidence provenance through the
autonomy loop" is the brief's candidate name. No novelty is assumed.

## 2. Incidents where provenance was the issue

| incident | provenance failure | level |

## 3. Assessment

- **Capturing provenance is established.** Data-flow provenance (L043,
  L044) and fielded reset provenance (L063-L065) exist.
- **Using action provenance to decide whether a piece of evidence is valid**
  is P02 (compensating actions) or P05 (estimator actions). Both classify as
  engineering.
- **As a separate research problem, provenance adds a record.** It does not
  add an inference method. The distinction between "evidence provenance
  through the autonomy loop" and decision provenance (L043) is largely
  **terminological**.

No directly matching work was identified in the searched sources that
attaches autonomy-action provenance to every monitor input in an autopilot
stack. The fielded reset counters (L063) come closest for the estimator.

## 4. Answer to key question 6

**Monitors in the searched systems represent action provenance for estimator
resets and switches (L063-L065). Model-based and formal monitors represent
it for commands (L012, L035, L037).**

- A general "evidence provenance through the autonomy loop" is **data-flow
  engineering** plus P02/P05.
- Candidate P08 is **ENGINEERING_ONLY**, N1.
