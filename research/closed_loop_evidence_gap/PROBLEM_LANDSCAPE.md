# Closed-loop evidence endogeneity: problem landscape

Closed-loop evidence gap search, 2026-09-28. This package is problem-first. It
does not design or implement anything, and it states no candidate problem in
terms of CSIA.

"Closed-loop evidence endogeneity" is the brief's **working name** for the
phenomenon below. It is not treated as an established term, and nothing in
this package claims the phenomenon is new.

## 1. The phenomenon, stated precisely

The brief's chain:

1. The autonomy stack holds state or evidence **E**.
2. It takes action **A** because of E. A may come from:
   - a controller;
   - an allocator;
   - an estimator (reset, exclusion, lane switch, covariance change);
   - a planner;
   - a runtime-assurance switch;
   - a monitor's own escalation.
3. A changes the physical or computational process that generates later
   evidence **E'**.
4. A monitor reasons from E' as if E' were generated by a process that does
   not depend on A.

The question is **not** whether evidence changes over time. It is whether
the **meaning, distribution, validity or identifiability** of E' depends on
the stack's own earlier actions, and whether monitors and assurance
arguments account for that dependence.

## 4. Classification of the problem

Of the four options in the brief (KNOWN AND SOLVED, KNOWN BUT OPEN,
FRAGMENTED ACROSS FIELDS, GENUINELY UNDERDEVELOPED):

**FRAGMENTED ACROSS FIELDS, with each fragment KNOWN.**

- **Each fragment has its own mature theory:**
  - closed-loop identification;
  - integrated control and diagnosis;
  - dual control and active diagnosis;
  - g-methods;
  - performative prediction;
  - ARAIM integrity after exclusion.
- **Most fragments are solved in principle.**
- **The open parts are known and crowded:**
  - real-time safe active diagnosis for high-dimensional nonlinear systems
    (L008, L009);
  - monitoring under performativity (L026).
- **A common formalism already exists.** All of the fragments are the
  sequential interventional distribution of evidence given the action
  history:
  - Robins' g-formula (L022) in statistics;
  - the dual effect and POMDP belief dynamics (L013, L034) in control.

  A cross-field "theory of endogenous evidence" would largely rename it
  (`PRIOR_ART_KILL_MATRIX.csv`, P13).
- **It is not genuinely underdeveloped** in the searched sources. The
  unevenness is in **fielded modular stacks**:
  - PX4 and ArduPilot propagate estimator-reset provenance to controllers
    (L063-L065);
  - but monitors in the files read reuse evidence across their own or other
    modules' interventions without explicit validity accounting (L066,
    L068).

  That is engineering (`TOP_GAPS.md`).

## 5. Where XSYS-001 sits

- **What was compared.**
  - The XSYS-001 dynamics witness compares vehicle response with the nominal
    **closed-loop** response envelope (L070). It is not a plant-model
    residual driven by the actual commands.
  - The compensation witness W_CTRL is a controller-output observation
    (L070).
- **Textbook form.** Read this way, the late-window failure (L069) is the
  classic closed-loop form of masking:
  - the fault leaves the output once compensation settles;
  - it persists in the control signal (L002, L014, L021).
- **What XSYS-001 contributes.** A clean, preregistered, blinded,
  matched-set **quantification** of that known mechanism in one simulated
  configuration (55/55 wrong when read outside the window).
- **What it does not show:**
  - that validity depends on the **action history** rather than on time
    since onset (the controller was fixed and the window was time-indexed);
  - that an input-conditioned plant residual would also fail;
  - any hardware behaviour.

It is an example, not unique evidence (`TOP_GAPS.md`, item G of the report).

**Conclusion.**

- The phenomenon is **general**. It is not limited to fault-tolerant control
  masking a fault.
- In every domain where a consequence is documented, a domain theory
  already exists.
- For power, communications and compute, the masking itself was not
  documented in the searched sources (**UNCLEAR**).
