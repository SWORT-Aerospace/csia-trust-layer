whether or not you use the analyzer for that log. The experimenter tells
you, per log, which tools you may use.

**Answer from the log, not from what you expect.** "Cannot tell from this
log" is a valid answer, and it is scored as correct where it is correct.

## Why these questions

**Q1-Q4 and Q7 are the engineer's questions** from the analyzer's first
page. **Q5 and Q6 test** whether evidence quality and evidence dependence
were noticed. **Q8 tests** whether stated certainty matches what the log
can support. **Q9 tests** reproducibility. None of them asks whether the
tool was liked; that is the feedback form.
