## 2. Recovering the instantaneous residual

`td.physics["gps"]` is **already smoothed**: the comparator computes
`lam = ew_smooth(chi2, α = 0.15)`. No unsmoothed λ exists anywhere in the
pipeline's output, so "instantaneous versus smoothed" could not be read off
directly. `ew_smooth` is a first-order IIR,

    y_t = (1 − α) y_{t−1} + α x_t     ⟹     x_t = ( y_t − (1 − α) y_{t−1} ) / α

so the instantaneous chi-square is recovered **exactly** by inversion. The
script re-smooths the recovered series and aborts the run if the round-trip
error exceeds 1e-9 rather than reporting a reconstruction as a measurement.
Measured round-trip error: 0 in all four arms.

## 4. Crossing latencies, seconds after onset

| arm | instantaneous λ | smoothed λ | trust | CUSUM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `clean` | 2.92 † | 24.88 † | **none** | **none** |
| `slow_ramp` (1 m/s) | 0.44 | 0.56 | 8.72 | 2.96 |
| `spoof_3ms` (3 m/s) | 0.20 | 0.28 | 3.36 | 1.16 |
| `fast_ramp` (6 m/s) | 0.16 | 0.20 | 3.24 | 0.72 |

† **These are false alarms, not latencies.** The clean arm has no fault, so
any crossing of the bare λ threshold is a false positive. The JSON carries
`ground_truth_faulted: false` and `crossings_are_false_alarms: true` on that
row so the numbers cannot be lifted out of context.

## 5. What the numbers say

**Smoothing costs latency, and the cost shrinks as the fault gets faster.**
+0.12 s at 1 m/s, +0.08 s at 3 m/s, +0.04 s at 6 m/s. That is not a fixed lag:
a fixed lag would be constant. A faster ramp drives the residual through the
threshold before the smoother's memory matters.

**Smoothing buys a 4× reduction in false crossings.** In the pre-onset window
(5.0–20.0 s, 374 ticks), identical in all four arms because it precedes onset:

| series | ticks over the λ threshold |
|---|---|
| instantaneous λ | **36** |
| smoothed λ | **9** |
| trust below `nav_caution` | **0** |
| CUSUM alarming | **0** |

**The bare λ threshold is not a usable detector, and reporting its latency
alone would flatter it.** It fires 36 times in clean flight before onset and
crosses again on the clean arm after t = 20 s with no fault present. The two
paths the layer actually uses — trust and CUSUM — produce **zero** false
alarms in the clean window and **no** crossing at all on the clean arm.

**CUSUM beats the trust threshold in every faulted arm**: 2.96 vs 8.72 s,
1.16 vs 3.36 s, 0.72 vs 3.24 s. The gap is largest on the slowest ramp
(5.76 s), which is where accumulation is worth most.

## 6. Prior art, and no novelty claim

**No CUSUM novelty is claimed.** The detector is Page's 1954 one-sided CUSUM,
already labelled as prior art inside `csia/v1_1/sequential.py`'s own
docstring, promoted from an ablation harness because it measured well, not
because it is interesting. `k = 0.5` is textbook; `h = 500` was fixed by a
zero-clean-alarm rule on a grid before these four arms existed, and the
textbook `h = 5.0` is retained as a committed **negative** result (2681 clean
alarm ticks).

The established literature this sits in — cumulative innovations, sequential
change detection, sequential monitoring — is the frame for the result, not
something the result extends. `research/math-paper/PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.csv` row
`Page 1954` carries status `KNOWN`.

**What may be claimed:** the *measurement* — that on this deployed layer, with
these frozen constants, accumulation converts a 8.72 s trust latency into a
2.96 s alarm on a 1 m/s ramp while adding zero clean-flight false alarms. That
is an engineering measurement of a known detector on a specific system.

## 7. Limits, stated with the result

- **Four arms, one flight profile, one node.** `gps` only. The CUSUM bank has
  no residual for `imu0`, `imu1` or `baro` at all, stated in
  `sequential.py` `NO_RESIDUAL`, so "no alarm" must never be read as "no
  fault" for those nodes.
- **Bench simulator, not a real autopilot.** These arms are
  `csia/scenarios.py`'s pipeline, not SITL. The current-stack question is in
  `CURRENT_STACK_BLOCKERS.md`.
- **The λ threshold is a diagnostic device for this comparison**, chosen as
  μ₀ + 3σ₀ so both series could be compared in the same units. It is not a
  threshold the system uses, and §5 shows why it should not be.
- **Ground truth is the injection schedule.** That is what makes the false-
  alarm counts meaningful and is the reason the same counts cannot be produced
  on public logs (see `PUBLIC_LOG_STUDY_PLAN.md`).
