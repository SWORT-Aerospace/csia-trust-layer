## 0. What the verification of the prior result actually found

Every prior locked value listed for checking was reproduced from
`results/RCEC_LOCKED_CAMPAIGN.json` by an independently written reduction. Four
things came out differently from the way they were written down, and the
repository value governs.

**0.1 The "90% resolution frontier" is a margin statement, not a certificate
statement.** The stored `resolvable_fraction` is `M_R > 0`. It does not require
`CERTIFIED_IN_MODEL`. Under the stricter predicate every frontier is one grid
step worse and one arm disappears entirely:

| arm | published frontier (`M_R > 0`) | frontier requiring `CERTIFIED_IN_MODEL` |
|---|---|---|
| P0 passive | never | never |
| P1 palindromic | 0.020 | 0.040 |
| P2 compensated | **0.006** | 0.010 |
| P3 shaped | 0.010 | 0.020 |
| P4 feedback | 0.010 | 0.020 |
| P5 random-safe | 0.020 | 0.040 |
| P6 mission | never | never |
| P7 (old) | 0.040 | **never** |

Both are computed here (`frontier_margin_positive`,
`frontier_certified_and_positive`). The published numbers are correct for the
predicate that was used; the label "resolution frontier" oversold it. This
phase reports the predicate with the number, always.

**0.2 "Zero false certificates in 8640 runs" measured nothing.** The check in
`tools/research/rcec_locked_campaign.py::false_certificates` is
`state == CERTIFIED and M_R < 0`. But `rcec.certify` returns `CERTIFIED` only
on the branch where `achieved < margin`, i.e. exactly when `M_R > 0`. The set
is empty by construction. It is a code invariant, not an empirical soundness
result, and it is **withdrawn as evidence of soundness**. Building a check that
can actually fail is the reason Layer B exists.

Consequently the sentence in `RESULTS_LOCKED.md` §7 — "Separating plants
requires scoring the estimate against plant truth, which only the
false-certificate check does here" — is also wrong. Nothing in that campaign
scored against plant truth.

**0.3 The paired-win denominators are per-plant, not campaign-wide.** 140/140
and 240/240 are the one-RC slice and reproduce exactly, including the median
differences (+0.0391840889243677 and +0.006057004416076926 ohm). Campaign-wide
the same comparisons are 420/420 and 726/726 (median +0.0391840889243677 and
+0.006061964879509852). The win *rate* is 100% either way. The `simple` plant
gives 246, not 240, for P2 vs P1, because P1 produced a margin in six more
cells there.

**0.4 The plant-margin identity is stronger than reported.** The margins are
not merely equal to twelve decimal places across the three plants in 2012
cells — they are **bit-identical in all 2012**. `M_R^cert` is a function of the
realized current and the declared envelope and never reads the plant's voltage
response. The withdrawal of the simple-vs-one-RC comparison was correct and is
now explained rather than merely asserted.

**0.5 Verified unchanged.** Energy 45.85341 A·s (P2) against 40.74283 A·s (P1);
certificate counts 4723 / 1440 / 1320 / 620 / 537 summing to 8640; clipping →
`CLIPPED_CERTIFICATE_INVALID` and abort → `ABORTED_INCOMPLETE`; 0.40 V ringing →
`UNKNOWN_OR_MODEL_MISMATCH` while 0.10 V is absorbed as `CERTIFIED_IN_MODEL`;
only lag ratio 1.0 certifies in the locked sweep and a fourfold lag
underestimate (ratio 0.25) flips the realized lever sign to +1; elevated drift
collapses P2 and P3 to never while P4 holds 0.010; the polarization envelope is
105 enumerated points with 1800 sampled draws and the rule-of-three statement
`p < 3/1800 ≈ 0.00167`; frozen core 12/12 files match.

**0.6 Two process defects, recorded not fixed in place.**
`RAW_CHECKSUMS.sha256` pins nine living source and document files alongside the
immutable raw JSON; those nine have legitimately moved on since MX-039 and the
manifest now fails on them. A checksum file that mixes immutable artifacts with
files expected to change carries no signal. The raw campaign JSON itself still

it is `sha256(json.dumps(FROZEN_BASELINE.json["files"], sort_keys=True))`, now
pinned in `tools/research/iv_base_provenance.py`.

## 1. Hypotheses

**H1 — independent outcome advantage.** At matched duration, matched energy
class and identical safety constraints, at least one deployable active method
among P2, P3, P4 improves the *independent* ground-truth outcome metric
`M_R^out` relative to all of P0, P1, P5 and P6, on the primary comparison
defined in §4.

**H2 — nominal/robust tradeoff.** P2 has the best nominal grid resolution area
`A_s`, while P4 has a strictly better worst-case `A_s` taken over elevated
drift and lag-estimation error.

**H3 — robust selection.** A selector using only pre-action and
online-observable nuisance estimates achieves a worst-case `A_s` at least as
good as the better of always-P2 and always-P4, without increasing the count of
false certificates as measured by Layer B.

**H4 — certificate calibration.** Conditional on `CERTIFIED_IN_MODEL`, the
independent outcome margin `M_R^out` is positive in at least 90% of cells
inside the declared model envelope.

**H5 — plant discrimination.** `M_R^out` differs across Q0, Q1 and Q2 in cells
where `M_R^cert` is bit-identical. (Given §0.4, `M_R^cert` is bit-identical
across plants by construction, so this hypothesis is about whether the *new*
evaluator has any plant sensitivity at all. If it does not, the evaluator is a
failed instrument, not a finding.)

**H6 — open-set boundary.** There exists a measurable separation `δ*` in the
preregistered normalized observation metric such that unknown-fault
trajectories with `δ_unknown > δ*` route to `UNKNOWN_OR_MODEL_MISMATCH` or
`MODEL_MISMATCH_LIMITED` at a rate at least 90%, and trajectories with
`δ_unknown` well below `δ*` do not.

**H7 — commanded information is insufficient.** There exists a causal, bounded,
admissible command-to-current transformation `F` under which the command-space
conditions `S_II(u) ≥ S_II^min` and `|S_tI(u)|/S_II(u) ≤ γ` both hold while the
realized current `I = F(u)` violates at least one of them.

## 2. Frozen constants

These are fixed now and may not be changed after any outcome is seen. If one of
them turns out to be badly chosen, the correct response is to report the
experiment under the frozen constant and say so, not to re-choose it.

| constant | value | why this value, decided before any outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `TAU_R_RELATIVE` | `0.5` | Primary accuracy requirement: `τ_R(d) = 0.5·d`. The magnitude grid is spaced by roughly a factor of two, so an error of half the claimed change is the loosest requirement under which the estimate still has the right sign and cannot be confused with the neighbouring grid point. Derived from the experiment's own grid, not from a hardware specification. |
| `TAU_R_ABSOLUTE` | `0.002` ohm | Secondary, reported alongside: the smallest magnitude on the grid. An estimator whose change-error exceeds the smallest change it is asked to detect supports no decision anywhere on the grid. |
| `COVERAGE_TARGET` | `0.90` | Matches the 90% convention already used for the resolution frontier, so the two are comparable. |
| `RESOLVE_PREDICATE` | `M_R^out > 0` | Definition of "resolved" in `A_s`. Stated with every number, per §0.1. |
| primary interaction statistic | `I_s` (§4.2) | Finite, defined when a method never resolves, unlike the withdrawn scalar. |
| magnitude grid `D` | `0.002, 0.004, 0.006, 0.010, 0.020, 0.040` ohm | Same grid as the locked campaign, so the two phases are comparable. Concentrated across the 0.004-0.020 transition with outer controls at both ends. |
| `d_max_tested` | `0.040` ohm | Censoring bound. A frontier above this is reported as `> 0.040`, never as infinity. |

`τ_R` is preregistered and synthetic. It is a property of this simulation's
grid. It is **not** an application requirement, because there is no application
here; calling it one would be inventing a number.

## 3. Layer separation, and what each layer may see

**Layer A — deployable certificate.** `csia_active_evidence.rcec`,
`.probes`, `.rcec_selector`, `.realized_moments`, `.waveform_compensation`,
`.moment_feedback`, `.polarization`, `.identifiability`, `.authority`. May see:
declared configuration and uncertainty envelope, proposed and authorised
command, measured current/voltage/timestamps, coverage and status flags,
permitted online nuisance estimates, provenance hashes. May **not** see: true
fault labels, true `R_0`, true RC state, hidden plant parameters,
counterfactual trajectories, or any Layer B output. Layer A is not modified by
this phase.

**Layer B — independent outcome evaluator.**
`csia_independent_validation.evaluator`, `.truth`, `.plants`, `.ceilings`.
Offline. May see ground truth. **May not import or call**
`csia_active_evidence.rcec`'s resolution-margin computation. Enforced by an
architectural test over transitive imports, plus mutation tests that
deliberately leak truth into Layer A and must fail.

**Layer C — experimental auditor.** `csia_independent_validation.audit`.
Checks partitions, RNG stream alignment, cost matching, artifact integrity,
leakage, and whether the stated conclusions follow from the preregistered
metrics.

One-way data flow: A → B → C. Never the reverse.

## 4. Metrics, defined before they are computed

### 4.1 Independent outcome metrics

For a point estimate of series resistance, with `R_0,true` taken from the
simulator's own state and not from any estimator:

- `e_R = R̂_0 − R_0,true`
- `AE_R = |e_R|`
- `RE_R = |e_R| / max(|R_0,true|, ε)`, `ε = 1e-9`

For change estimation, across a matched nominal/fault pair driven by common
random numbers:

- `e_ΔR = (R̂_fault − R̂_nominal) − (R_fault − R_nominal)`
- `M_R^out = τ_R(d) − |e_ΔR|`, with `τ_R(d) = 0.5·d` (primary)

For interval or set estimates: truth coverage, interval width, nominal/fault
set separation, false exclusion of the true hypothesis, simultaneous coverage
over the full window when claimed, and calibration stratified by plant and by
nuisance level.

`M_R^cert` and `M_R^out` are reported separately and never substituted for one
another. Their calibration relationship is itself a result.

### 4.2 Censoring-aware comparison

Grid resolution area over the frozen magnitude grid `D`:

```
A_s = (1/|D|) · Σ_j P_s(resolve | d_j)
```

Finite drift interaction, primary:

```
I_s = (A_{s,drift} − A_{P0,drift}) − (A_{s,no drift} − A_{P0,no drift})
```

Censored frontier: the smallest magnitude reaching 90% resolution when
observed, otherwise the literal string `> 0.040`. Never infinity, never
arithmetic on it. All three of `A_s`, the paired median `M_R^out` difference,
and the censored frontier are reported together.

## 5. Falsifiers, declared in advance

This phase is falsified, in whole or in the named part, if any of the following
holds. Each is a result to be reported, not a defect to be repaired.

1. Active methods lose their advantage under the independent evaluator.
2. P2's apparent gain is explained by greater energy or duration under the
   matched-cost analysis.
3. The robust selector is found to use ground truth, future information,
   strategy labels, or final outcomes.
4. P4's robustness disappears outside the exact locked grid.
5. Certificate-positive cases have independent coverage below `COVERAGE_TARGET`.
6. Sensing defects remain absent from the outcome metric because the evaluator
   forgot them rather than because they cancel.
7. The richer plant Q2 changes no outcome because it is not actually connected
   to the generated voltage.
8. Unknown faults are forced into known labels.
9. P7b is beaten by a deployable strategy on P7b's own objective, action space,
   plant instance and constraints. This invalidates the oracle implementation
   and stops the phase; the deployable method is **not** relabelled
   super-oracular.
10. The result depends on one seed, one phase, one lag, or one hand-picked fault
    magnitude.
11. Any new final-partition seed is exposed during development.
12. Any old locked artifact or historical claim is modified.

## 6. Conditions for opening the sealed final partition

The `final` partition stays closed until **all** of the following are true, each
demonstrated by a committed artifact:

1. all instrument controls pass, positive and negative, in the same run;
2. plant independence is demonstrated (`PLANT_INDEPENDENCE_AUDIT.md`);
3. the P7b ceiling invariant passes on development **and** validation;
4. no truth leakage is possible through imports or data schemas, proven by the
   architectural tests and by mutation tests that must fail;
5. the primary finite interaction statistic is frozen (it is: `I_s`);
6. target accuracy and coverage requirements are frozen (they are: §2);
7. the runner has completed at full scale on the validation partition;
8. the working tree is clean;
9. the full test suite and the blocking/publication guards pass;
10. the frozen-core digests match.

If any condition fails, the phase reports what it has and does not open the
seal. The seal is not opened because a prompt asked for one.
