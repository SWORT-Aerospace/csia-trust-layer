# MAER 4A Reopen — Raw GNSS Receiver-Health Audit

**Verdict: `MAER_STRUCTURAL_RESCUE_REOPENED`.**

**The prior claim — "under self-occlusion the only discriminating observable is
the absence event itself" — is `REFUTED`.** It was too strong, and the reason it
looked true is a scope error I made: the first audit measured the *normalized
CSIA channels*, then generalised to *the protocol*. Those are different claims.

Artifacts: `MAER_RAW_GNSS_AUDIT.json`, `MAER_RAW_GNSS_SURVIVAL.csv`, raw
captures `raw_gnss_probe_{ardupilot,px4}.json`, generated by
`maer_raw_gnss_probe.py` and `maer_raw_gnss_audit.py`. No frozen-core file was
touched, no MAER arm was implemented, no scored trial was run.

---

## 1. The scope error, stated plainly

Both adapters do this on every GNSS message:

```python
def _ingest_gps(self, g, t_src):
    fix = g("fix_type", 0)
    if fix is None or fix < 3:
        self._latest.pop("gnss.position", None)
        self._latest.pop("gnss.velocity", None)
        self._reject_sentinel(..., reason=MissingReason.NO_3D_FIX)
        return                      # <-- the WHOLE message is discarded here
```

`GPS_RAW_INT` also carries `satellites_visible`, `eph`, `epv`, `h_acc`,
`v_acc`, `vel_acc`, `hdg_acc`. **None of those is ever read** — a repository-wide
search for `satellites`, `eph`, `hdop` finds no adapter reference. So "the
channel is unusable during fix loss" was true and told us only about the
normalization layer. Whether the *receiver* stops saying anything about itself
is a separate question, and it had never been measured.

## 2. Method

Existing raw artifacts were checked first and are insufficient: the flight logs
store normalized channels only (`autopilot` and `extra` carry no GNSS fields),
and the only `.BIN` dataflash logs in the repository belong to a different
Phase-2 campaign. So a minimal probe was run — **not a scored campaign** —
reproducing the exact established intervention:

| platform | intervention | simulator |
|---|---|---|
| ArduPilot | `SIM_GPS1_ENABLE = 0` | ArduCopter V4.8.0-dev@01a504b |
| PX4 | `SIM_GPS_USED = 0` | PX4-Autopilot@547e2ca, `sihsim_quadx` |

20 s nominal, then inject, then 25 s. `GPS_RAW_INT` (24), `GPS_STATUS` (25) and
`GPS2_RAW` (124) were each explicitly requested via `SET_MESSAGE_INTERVAL`, so
a later absence means "the vehicle does not send it", not "nobody asked".

**Messages are partitioned by their own `fix_type`, not by wall-clock phase.**
The injection takes a moment to propagate, so a phase window straddles the
transition and mixes valid samples into the invalid-fix statistics. RULE B is
satisfied on both platforms: 132 (ArduPilot) and 124 (PX4) messages observed at
`fix_type < 3`, spanning 24.99 s each.

## 3. Result: message survival

**`GPS_RAW_INT` keeps arriving on both platforms during invalid fix**, at
5.283 Hz (ArduPilot) and 4.963 Hz (PX4). Every field is present on every message
and none is set to its MAVLink unknown-sentinel. Outcome A ("message absent") is
refuted on both platforms.

`GPS_STATUS` (per-satellite PRN / SNR / elevation / azimuth) and `GPS2_RAW`
were requested and **never arrived on either platform**, nominal or faulted.
Those are genuinely absent, so per-satellite SNR and constellation geometry are
not available here at all.

## 4. Result: does the surviving message still *say* anything?

This is where the platforms diverge completely, and it is the finding.

### ArduPilot under `SIM_GPS1_ENABLE = 0` — present but **frozen**

| field | nominal | invalid fix (132 msgs) |
|---|---|---|
| `time_usec` | distinct = 68 | **distinct = 1** |
| `satellites_visible` | 0–10 | 3, distinct = 1 |
| `eph` | 121 | 121, distinct = 1 |
| `h_acc` | 0–300 | 300, distinct = 1 |
| `lat` / `lon` | varying | constant |

**The receiver's own timestamp never advances across 132 messages over 25 s.**
ArduPilot is re-transmitting the last sample verbatim with `fix_type` downgraded
to 1. Every field is "present and non-sentinel" and every field is a fossil.
`satellites_visible = 3` is not a live count of tracked satellites; it is the
value frozen at the instant the simulated receiver stopped.

A step does exist at onset (`satellites_visible` 10 → 3 as `fix_type` 6 → 1),
but it is simultaneous with, and degenerate with, the validity event. Per Item 7
that is **gate-event evidence**, not an independent discriminating residual.

### PX4 under `SIM_GPS_USED = 0` — present and **live**

| field | nominal | invalid fix (124 msgs) | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `time_usec` | distinct = 99 | **distinct = 124** | advancing |
| `satellites_visible` | 10 | **0** | 10 → 0 |
| `eph` | 70 | **10000** | ×143 |
| `epv` | 110 | **10000** | ×91 |
| `h_acc` | 900 | **100000** | ×111 |
| `v_acc` | 1780 | **100000** | ×56 |
| `vel_acc` | 400 | **100000** | ×250 |

The record is live — the timestamp advances on every message — and six
receiver-health fields move by one to two orders of magnitude at the fault.
These are continuous-valued quantities, not the validity event, and they are not
the invalidated position solution.

## 5. Candidate screen (Item 8)

One screen applied to both platforms: receiver health rather than the
invalidated solution; not an estimator output; present on > 50 % of invalid-fix
messages; non-sentinel; **record live** (timestamp advancing); and the value
changes at the fault.

| platform | candidate fields |
|---|---|
| ArduPilot (`ENABLE=0`) | **none** — fails the liveness test |
| PX4 (`SIM_GPS_USED=0`) | **`satellites_visible`, `eph`, `epv`, `h_acc`, `v_acc`, `vel_acc`** |

Proven diagnostic accuracy is deliberately **not** required at this stage.

## 6. Estimator-independence check

`GPS_RAW_INT` is the raw receiver message: PX4 populates it from the
`sensor_gps` uORB topic published by the GPS driver, ArduPilot from the AP_GPS
backend. Neither is an estimator output.

The independence contract bars `estimator.variances` **specifically** — EKF
innovation/variance, "CSIA must not consume the estimator it is meant to be
independent of". It says nothing about `GPS_RAW_INT` health fields, which are
sensor-side. The adapters simply never read them. **Contract-permitted.**

## 7. The ArduPilot result is injection-specific, not a platform limit

This matters and would be easy to misreport. ArduPilot SITL exposes native
parameters that model *degraded* receiver health rather than a stopped driver
(`libraries/SITL/SIM_GPS.cpp`):

```
SIM_GPS1_NUMSATS   satellite count (default 10)
SIM_GPS1_JAM       simulated jamming — simulate_jamming() varies satellite
                   count, velocity, vz and position over time on randomised
                   intervals
SIM_GPS1_ACC       reported accuracy (default 0.3)
SIM_GPS1_FIXTYPE   reported fix type (default 6)
SIM_GPS1_LCKTIME   lock acquisition time
```

`SIM_GPS1_ENABLE = 0` — the injection Mechanism A chose — kills the driver, and
killing the driver is why the message freezes. A `JAM` or `NUMSATS` injection
would produce live degraded health metadata on ArduPilot too.

So the correct statement is: **under the specific fix-loss injection used for
Mechanism A, ArduPilot emits no live receiver-health information; the platform
is capable of emitting it under other native fault models.**

## 8. Matched ungated/gated pair (Item 10)

**Possible: YES**, on PX4.

| requirement | achievable | why |
|---|---|---|
| same input | yes | one field, e.g. `h_acc`, read once per tick |
| same transform | yes | one residual function in a research sidecar |
| same threshold | yes | one constant, shared by both arms |
| same hypothesis association | yes | both contribute to `gps_fault` |
| same normalization | yes | same adaptive-λ path |
| **only gate placement differs** | yes | ARM U uses the contribution ungated; ARM G multiplies the identical contribution by `gvalid` |

Nothing in the frozen core changes: the field is ingested by a research-only
sidecar, and the two arms differ by one multiplication. This is exactly the
causal control the first audit could not construct.

## 9. Honest caveats to carry into preregistration

1. **Tautology risk.** On PX4 these values are computed by `SensorGpsSim` as a
   direct function of the injected parameter. A residual on them may be
   detecting the simulator's fault model rather than a physical phenomenon.
   This is Reviewer 3 and Reviewer 7 territory and must be preregistered as a
   named limitation, with the ArduPilot `JAM` model as a partial cross-check.
2. **Platform asymmetry.** Mechanism A's primary platform is ArduPilot; the
   viable candidate is on PX4, which separately suffers transport starvation
   and has no second IMU. Either the injection changes on ArduPilot, or the
   structural arm runs on PX4 and platform is recorded as a factor.
3. **`satellites_visible = 0` is close to the validity event.** Zero satellites
   and no fix are near-degenerate. `h_acc` / `eph` are the better candidates:
   continuous, and in principle able to degrade *before* fix is lost.
4. **No per-satellite data anywhere.** `GPS_STATUS` never arrives, so SNR and
   constellation geometry — two of the originally listed candidate classes —
   remain unavailable on both platforms.
5. **The probe flew no mission.** It reproduces the injection and the message
   path, not the full flight condition. Field survival is a property of the
   receiver→driver→MAVLink path, but a scored campaign must confirm it in
   flight.

## 10. Verdict on the prior wording (Item 12)

> "under self-occlusion the only discriminating observable is the absence event
> itself"

**`REFUTED`.**

On PX4, six continuous-valued, live, estimator-independent, contract-permitted
receiver-health fields change by one to two orders of magnitude at the fault
while `GPS_RAW_INT` continues to arrive. They are not the absence event.

The claim survives only in the narrow form: *under the `SIM_GPS1_ENABLE = 0`
injection on ArduPilot, the raw message freezes, so the only information
distinguishing fault from nominal is the validity event and the step
coincident with it.* Stated for that platform-and-injection pair it is
supported. Stated generally it was wrong, and I generalised from the
normalization layer to the protocol without measuring the protocol.

## 11. What does NOT change

The first audit's other findings stand, because none depended on the raw-field
question:

- `CAUSES["gps_fault"] == ["phys_gps"]`, and `phys_gps` is triply destroyed by
  fix loss. A rescue is an **addition**, not a repair of the existing feature.
- Gate-event likelihood remains uncalibratable: 2 flights in the corpus contain
  any `NO_3D_FIX` tick.
- CUSUM (4C) and transport starvation (4D) remain PASS; operating regime (4E)
  remains DEFERRED.
- `estimator.variances` remains barred.

The overall MAER decision is **no longer `MAER_NOT_FEASIBLE`** on the strength
of 4A, but the experiment is **not yet cleared to run**: Item 10 stops at
"matched pair possible", and preregistration must come first.
