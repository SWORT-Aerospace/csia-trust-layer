# Producer health, contracts, and contract failures (Phases 6, 7 and 8)

**2026-10-01. Code:** `csia_evidence_assurance/health.py`, `contract.py`,
`contracts/*.json`. **Tests:** `test_contract_failure_taxonomy` (14 cases),
`test_a_malformed_contract_is_refused_at_load`,
`test_impossible_metadata_faults_the_producer_for_the_whole_tick_only`,
`test_in_process_producer_must_be_valid`.

## 1. Producer health (Phase 6)

**Health describes the producer; the record describes the world.** The two
are never merged.

| health | meaning | admissibility |
|---|---|---|
| VALID | working as its contract says | admitted, if everything else holds |
| DEGRADED | working, with a stated limitation (PX4: `time_slip` grew) | admitted, with the warning PRODUCER_DEGRADED |
| UNAVAILABLE | produced no meaningful output (PX4 before alignment; CUSUM gated out or not fresh) | PRODUCER_INVALID |
| FAULTED | the producer itself failed (PX4 `filter_fault_flags` 0-15; CUSUM non-finite statistic; impossible metadata) | PRODUCER_INVALID |
| UNKNOWN | a producer that must report health did not | PRODUCER_INVALID (PRODUCER_HEALTH_NOT_REPORTED) |

**UNKNOWN is kept.** Its meaning is real and operational: "this producer
should have told us and did not". An in-process producer (`IN_PROCESS`) must
be VALID, because the frozen plugin runtime already turns its failures into
UNAVAILABLE records.

**The case the brief named.** "Detector says NOMINAL / producer is FAULTED"
never becomes healthy aircraft evidence:
- the output is withheld;
- if no admitted output answers the same question, 2.1.1 receives it as
  UNAVAILABLE with its content cleared;
- an UNAVAILABLE record cannot make a component affirmatively nominal (C1).

Pinned by the Phase 22 tests and dataset G.

## 2. The contract (Phase 7)

**A contract is a small versioned JSON file:** schema
`csia.evidence_contract/1`. It is not a formal language and not a schema
framework. Every field has a reader:

| field | read by |
|---|---|
| `producer_id`, `producer_version`, `contract_version` | admissibility (version checks) |
| `claims`: meaning, domain, relation, attributable, `config_dependency` | admissibility (claim and record checks, epoch stamp); the report |
| `lineage`: fixed roots, or `PER_OUTPUT` | admissibility (LINEAGE_NOT_AS_DECLARED) |
| `update`: SNAPSHOT or SAMPLE_AND_HOLD, period | the Analyzer's hold |
| `validity`: `max_content_age_s`, or `event_window_s` relative to a named event | admissibility (STALE, REGIME_INVALID) |
| `health_reporting`: REPORTED or IN_PROCESS | admissibility |
| `profile_compatibility` | admissibility (PROFILE_MISMATCH) |
| `expected_inputs`, `fault_behavior`, `*_rule`, `source_reference` | the engineer; not read at runtime |

**A malformed contract is refused when it is loaded,** never at a tick.

**Contracts in this phase:**

| file | producer | how written |
|---|---|---|
| `cusum_v1_1_sequential.json` | the frozen v1.1 CUSUM | by hand, from the frozen constants |
| `px4_ekf2_estimator_status.json` | PX4 EKF2 status | by hand, with V4 line references to PX4 547e2ca |
| `xsys_w_rpm.json`, `xsys_w_dyn.json`, `xsys_w_ctrl.json` | the XSYS-001 witnesses | generated by `xsys_witness.contracts_dict()`; a test checks the files equal it |
| (in memory) `v2-domain:*` | the frozen v2 domain plugins | derived from each plugin's own declaration |

## 3. Contract failures (Phase 8)

**Principle:** one malformed producer never fails the tick. Its outputs are
withheld; the rest of the tick is reasoned as usual.

| failure | status | decision | why that and not stricter |
|---|---|---|---|
| wrong producer version | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT the output | its meaning may differ (L9) |
| wrong contract version | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | same |
| missing or invalid lineage | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | independence cannot be judged |
| unknown claim | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | no declared meaning (L6) |
| claim mapped to another record, or another attributability | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | the record would not mean what the claim says |
| lineage other than the declared one | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | — |
| profile mismatch | PROFILE_MISMATCH | REJECT | the claim was declared against another profile's components |
| missing epoch / undeclared epoch key | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | the configuration it depends on cannot be checked |
| malformed timestamp (not finite) | CONTRACT_INVALID | REJECT | freshness cannot be judged |
| VALID or DEGRADED with impossible metadata (content after production, production or content in the future) | PRODUCER_INVALID | **FAULT the producer for the tick**: every output of that producer that tick is withheld (PRODUCER_FAULTED_THIS_TICK) | a producer that misreports its own times cannot be trusted for its other outputs of the same tick; it is not blamed beyond the tick |
| health not reported | PRODUCER_INVALID | REJECT | — |
| producer DEGRADED | ADMISSIBLE | WARN (PRODUCER_DEGRADED on the result) | its contract says the output is still usable |
| malformed contract | refused at load | the producer is not configured | a contract is checked once |

**Not chosen:** failing the whole tick on any malformed producer. No producer
in this phase is indispensable. A missing producer's conclusions become less
certain through 2.1.1's own handling of evidence that could not look.
