# TIMING — EVIDENCE CONTRACT (Phase C §3)

## OUTPUT_STALE — `TIMING/clock_inconsistent`

**Observable symptom.** a timed channel's output is later than its function requires, or its timestamps are inconsistent

**Mechanism.** the timebase itself is wrong, so no latency computed from these stamps means anything

**Alternative explanations (other domains that can produce the same symptom).**

- `SENSOR/source_slow`
- `COMMUNICATION/transport_delay`
- `COMPUTE/processing_delay`
- `COMPUTE/process_stall`

**Required distinguishing evidence.**

| direction | predicate | reads |
|---|---|---|
| confirms | `CLOCK_CONSISTENT_FALSE` | TIMING |
| refutes | `CLOCK_CONSISTENT` | TIMING |

## POSITION_UNAVAILABLE — `TIMING/deadline_violation`

**Observable symptom.** a position or navigation function is not fully available

**Mechanism.** the deadline was missed with no segment over its own budget -- a timing fault in its own right

**Alternative explanations (other domains that can produce the same symptom).**

- `SENSOR/navigation_source_invalid`
- `COMPUTE/estimator_stale`
- `COMMUNICATION/starvation`

**Required distinguishing evidence.**

| direction | predicate | reads |
|---|---|---|
| confirms | `FUNCTION_TIMING_WITHIN_DEADLINE_FALSE` | TIMING |
| refutes | `FUNCTION_TIMING_WITHIN_DEADLINE` | TIMING |
| refutes | `NO_TIMING_SEGMENT_OVER_BUDGET_FALSE` | TIMING |

## FUNCTION_DEADLINE_MISS — `TIMING/budget_exceeded_without_segment`

**Observable symptom.** a function's end-to-end deadline was missed

**Mechanism.** every segment was inside its own budget and the total was not: the budgets do not sum to the deadline

**Alternative explanations (other domains that can produce the same symptom).**

- `COMMUNICATION/transport_delay`
- `COMPUTE/processing_delay`
- `SENSOR/source_slow`

**Required distinguishing evidence.**

| direction | predicate | reads |
|---|---|---|
| confirms | `TRANSPORT_WITHIN_BUDGET` | TIMING |
| confirms | `PROCESSING_WITHIN_BUDGET` | TIMING |
| confirms | `SOURCE_INTERVAL_NOMINAL` | TIMING |
| refutes | `TRANSPORT_WITHIN_BUDGET_FALSE` | TIMING |
| refutes | `PROCESSING_WITHIN_BUDGET_FALSE` | TIMING |
| refutes | `SOURCE_INTERVAL_NOMINAL_FALSE` | TIMING |

Confirmation requires **all** of the confirming predicates, not any.
